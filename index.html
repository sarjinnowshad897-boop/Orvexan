THE VISIONARY

Sarjin Nowshad

Founder & Creative Director, Chittagong, Bangladesh

Orvexan Fashion was born out of a relentless pursuit of elegance and individual expression. Starting this journey at an early age in Chittagong, Sarjin Nowshad combined a passion for timeless aesthetics with modern tailoring. Every piece reflects a commitment to sophisticated luxury, meticulous craftsmanship, and the belief that true style is rooted in both character and tradition.
